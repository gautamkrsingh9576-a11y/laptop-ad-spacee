BEFORE WE STICK

A few fair
questions.

What exactly am I enquiring about?

A physical brand-sticker placement on my Samsung laptop. We’ll agree the size, position, duration and price before you commit.

Can I choose the sticker placement?

Yes, you can share your preference. The placement examples are illustrative; final positioning depends on the logo, sticker size and available space.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is discussed on enquiry, along with campaign duration and placement. Send your requirements through the form to start the conversation.

Are the brands shown already sponsors?

The images and video illustrate the concept. Logos shown do not imply sponsorship, endorsement or an existing paid partnership.

Are impressions or sales guaranteed?

No. This is an independent advertising experiment with no guaranteed impressions, leads or sales. We’ll discuss the intended usage before you decide.

What happens when I click Enquire?

The enquiry form opens in a new tab. Submit your brand details and requirements there to start a conversation.
